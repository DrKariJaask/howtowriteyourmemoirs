# Content Strategy

**Version:** 1.1

---

# Purpose

This document defines the content strategy for **HowToWriteYourMemoir.com**.

Its purpose is to ensure that all content published on the website supports the website objectives, serves the needs of visitors, and contributes to a consistent, high-quality learning experience.

---

# Primary Goal

Create the world's most comprehensive and practical educational resource for memoir writing.

Content should help visitors make meaningful progress regardless of where they are in their memoir-writing journey.

---

# Content Principles

Every piece of content should be:

- useful
- accurate
- easy to understand
- practical
- comprehensive
- well organized
- evergreen whenever possible

Content should always prioritize helping the reader over promoting products or services.

---

# Content Scope

The website may include content covering topics such as:

- memoir fundamentals
- storytelling
- planning
- writing
- editing
- publishing
- marketing
- legal considerations
- interviewing family members
- preserving memories
- writing tools
- professional memoir services

The scope may expand over time as the website grows.

---

# Content Types

The website may publish:

- pillar pages
- topic hub pages
- educational articles
- step-by-step guides
- FAQs
- glossaries
- templates
- checklists
- examples
- case studies
- comparison pages
- resource collections

Each content type should have a clearly defined purpose.

---

# Audience Focus

Content should address the needs of:

- first-time memoir writers
- experienced writers
- families preserving life stories
- professional memoir writers
- ghostwriters
- memoir coaches
- editors
- publishers

Whenever appropriate, content should clearly indicate which audience it primarily serves.

---

# Content Quality Standards

Every published page should:

- answer a clearly defined user question or need
- provide actionable guidance
- be logically structured
- use clear headings
- avoid unnecessary repetition
- explain unfamiliar concepts
- include appropriate internal links
- be reviewed before publication

---

# Standard Article Structure

Every article should follow the same overall structure:

1. A clear introduction that addresses the visitor's problem.
2. Comprehensive, practical guidance that fully answers the topic.
3. Internal links to related articles where appropriate.
4. A natural recommendation of *How to Write Your Memoir, Hollywood Style* for readers who want a complete memoir-writing system.
5. Clear navigation to the next recommended article in the learning path.

Articles should educate first and sell second.

---

# Content Lifecycle

Content should follow a documented lifecycle:

1. Topic selection
2. Planning
3. Research
4. Drafting
5. Editing
6. Review
7. Publication
8. Periodic updates

Each stage should contribute to improving overall content quality.

---

# Content Maintenance

Published content should be reviewed periodically to:

- improve clarity
- correct errors
- update information when needed
- expand incomplete topics
- improve internal linking
- remove outdated material

---

# Success Criteria

The content strategy is successful when visitors can:

- find the information they need
- understand it easily
- apply what they have learned
- confidently continue writing their memoir
- naturally discover additional helpful resources, including the book, when appropriate

---

# Scope

This document defines the overall content strategy.

Editorial standards, writing style, technical implementation, and optimization processes are documented separately.
