# Editorial Guidelines

**Version:** 1.1

---

# Purpose

This document defines the editorial standards for **HowToWriteYourMemoir.com**.

Its purpose is to ensure that all published content is consistent, trustworthy, easy to understand, and aligned with the website's objectives and brand strategy.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

Every article should genuinely help the reader.

Content should educate, guide, and encourage visitors rather than simply attract traffic.

The primary measure of quality is whether the content helps someone make meaningful progress in writing a memoir.

---

# Editorial Principle

The primary purpose of every article is to help the reader.

Book recommendations should feel like useful guidance rather than sales messages.

The website should become the internet's most trusted source for memoir-writing advice.

Educational value always takes priority over promotion.

---

# Writing Principles

All content should be:

- accurate
- clear
- practical
- honest
- encouraging
- well structured
- reader-focused

Avoid unnecessary complexity whenever simpler language communicates the same idea.

---

# Accuracy

Authors should:

- verify factual statements whenever appropriate
- distinguish facts from opinions
- avoid unsupported claims
- acknowledge uncertainty when necessary
- correct errors promptly when discovered

---

# Readability

Content should:

- use short paragraphs
- use descriptive headings
- explain unfamiliar terms
- present ideas in a logical order
- use examples when they improve understanding

Readers should never feel lost while reading.

---

# Tone

Content should be:

- professional
- friendly
- respectful
- supportive
- confident without exaggeration

The website should never criticize or discourage aspiring memoir writers.

---

# Inclusiveness

Content should respect readers from different:

- cultures
- backgrounds
- ages
- writing experience levels
- life experiences

Examples and language should be broadly understandable to an international audience.

---

# Transparency

Whenever recommendations are made:

- distinguish guidance from personal opinion
- disclose commercial relationships when relevant
- clearly separate educational content from promotional content

Trust should always take priority over marketing.

---

# Consistency

Editors should strive for consistency in:

- terminology
- formatting
- structure
- tone
- capitalization
- spelling
- citation style

Readers should experience the website as one coherent publication regardless of who created the content.

---

# Editorial Review

Before publication, content should be reviewed for:

- clarity
- accuracy
- completeness
- readability
- consistency
- grammar
- spelling
- formatting

Whenever possible, every page should receive at least one editorial review before publication.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Editorial guidelines should evolve as the website grows.

Significant changes to editorial standards should be documented in future versions of this document.

---

# Scope

This document defines editorial standards only.

Content strategy, visual style, technical implementation, and optimization processes are documented separately.
