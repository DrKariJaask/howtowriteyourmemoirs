# Domain Selection

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document explains the rationale for selecting the domain name of the website.

The objective is to ensure that the chosen domain supports the long-term goals of the project, aligns with the brand strategy, and serves the needs of the target audience.

---

# Requirements

The domain should:

- clearly communicate the website's purpose
- be easy to understand
- be easy to remember
- be easy to spell
- work internationally
- support long-term brand development
- accurately reflect the primary topic of the website

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Candidate domains should be evaluated against the following criteria:

- Clarity
- Memorability
- Relevance
- Brand potential
- International usability
- Search intent alignment
- Long-term sustainability

---

# Selected Domain

**HowToWriteYourMemoirs.com**

---

# Rationale

The selected domain was chosen because it:

- immediately communicates the purpose of the website
- targets a clear user intent
- describes the primary topic without ambiguity
- is understandable to an international audience
- aligns with the educational mission of the website
- supports future expansion of the brand

# Risks

Potential disadvantages of the selected domain include:

- relatively long name
- limited flexibility if the website later expands beyond memoir writing
- users may occasionally confuse "memoir" with "autobiography"

These risks were considered acceptable given the benefits of clarity and relevance.

---

# Future Review

The selected domain may be reconsidered only if:

- the strategic direction of the website changes significantly
- a superior domain becomes available
- branding considerations clearly outweigh the benefits of the current domain

Any future domain change should be documented together with the rationale.

---

# Scope
