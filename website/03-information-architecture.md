# Information Architecture

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the overall information architecture of HowToWriteYourMemoirs.com.

Its purpose is to organize the website into a logical, scalable, and user-centered structure that helps visitors quickly find the information they need while supporting future growth.

---

# Design Principles

The information architecture should:

- be intuitive
- minimize the number of clicks needed to reach useful information
- group related topics together
- support both beginners and experienced writers
- scale as new content is added
- make relationships between topics easy to understand

---

# Content Hierarchy

The website is organized into four levels.

## Level 1 – Homepage

The homepage introduces the website and directs visitors to the main content areas.

---

## Level 2 – Topic Hubs

Each major subject has a dedicated hub page.

Examples include:

- Getting Started
- Finding Your Story
- Planning Your Memoir
- Writing Techniques
- Editing
- Publishing
- Marketing
- Professional Services

Topic hubs provide an overview of the subject and link to related articles.

---

## Level 3 – Educational Articles

Each hub contains detailed articles that answer specific user questions.

Examples include:

- How to start writing a memoir
- Memoir vs autobiography
- How to structure a memoir
- Common memoir writing mistakes
- How long should a memoir be?

Articles should be interconnected where appropriate.

---

## Level 4 – Supporting Content

Supporting content may include:

- FAQs
- Checklists
- Templates
- Examples
- Resource pages
- Glossaries

These pages provide additional value and support the educational content.

---

# Navigation Principles

Navigation should:

- clearly indicate where the visitor is
- make important sections easy to reach
- expose related content
- encourage logical progression through the writing journey
- avoid unnecessary complexity

---

# Internal Linking

Internal links should:

- connect closely related topics
- guide visitors to the next logical step
- reduce orphan pages
- reinforce the overall content structure
- improve content discoverability

Links should always provide value to the visitor.

---

# Scalability

The architecture should support future expansion without requiring major restructuring.

New topic hubs, articles, tools, and resources should fit naturally within the existing hierarchy.

---

# User Journey

The architecture should support visitors throughout the memoir-writing process, from initial curiosity to completing and publishing a memoir.

Visitors should always have a clear next step regardless of where they enter the website.

---

# Future Evolution

The architecture should be reviewed periodically as new content is published and user needs evolve.

Structural changes should prioritize usability and maintain logical organization.

---

# Scope

This document defines the structure of the website.

Individual content planning, editorial standards, visual design, and technical implementation are documented separately.
