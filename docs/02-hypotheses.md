# Hypotheses

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the hypotheses that will be evaluated during the study.

The hypotheses are established before the main observation period begins and are intended to distinguish predefined research questions from interpretations developed after data collection.

The purpose of this document is not to predict the outcome of the study but to define the questions the research seeks to answer.

---

# Primary Hypothesis

## H1

Documented website development and optimization activities are associated with measurable changes in website visibility over time.

---

# Secondary Hypotheses

## H2

Different optimization activities may be associated with different patterns of change in website visibility.

---

## H3

The timing of observed changes in website visibility may differ between optimization activities.

---

## H4

Some optimization activities may have no measurable association with website visibility.

---

## H5

Different visibility metrics may respond differently to the same optimization activity.

---

# Exploratory Questions

In addition to the predefined hypotheses, the study explores questions that cannot be fully specified before observations begin.

Examples include:

- Which optimization activities appear to have the strongest observable relationship with visibility?
- Which metrics provide the most useful information during long-term monitoring?
- Which observations suggest new hypotheses for future studies?
- Which findings warrant further investigation?

Exploratory findings will be reported separately from hypothesis evaluation.

---

# Hypothesis Evaluation

Each hypothesis will be evaluated using the measurements collected during the study.

The evaluation will be based on documented observations and recorded measurements.

The results may:

- support a hypothesis
- partially support a hypothesis
- fail to support a hypothesis

---

# Interpretation

The evaluation of the hypotheses applies only to this study.

The results should not be interpreted as general conclusions beyond the scope of the collected evidence.

---

# Post Hoc Hypotheses

If new hypotheses arise during the study, they will be documented separately as post hoc hypotheses.

The original hypotheses defined in this document will remain unchanged.
