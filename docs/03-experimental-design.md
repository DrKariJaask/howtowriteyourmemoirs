# Experimental Design

---

# Experimental Units

The study evaluates website performance at multiple levels, including:

- the entire website
- individual pages
- groups of related pages
- individual optimization cycles

The level of analysis used for each observation will be documented where appropriate.

---

# Experimental Groups

## Baseline Website

The baseline website represents a conventional AI-assisted publishing workflow.

Content is created using ChatGPT together with standard editorial judgement and common SEO practices.

After publication, pages may only receive:

- factual corrections
- spelling or grammar corrections
- broken link fixes

No optimization decisions may be based on Targetlytics recommendations or AI visibility measurements.

---

## Targetlytics Website

The Targetlytics website follows the same overall publishing schedule and covers the same subject area as the baseline website.

However, all optimization decisions are guided by Targetlytics.

Targetlytics may use one or more AI models internally whenever doing so is expected to improve recommendation quality.

Examples include:

- topic selection
- content expansion
- internal linking
- entity optimization
- topical authority development
- AI recommendation optimization

---

# Controlled Variables

The study attempts to keep the following factors as similar as reasonably possible:

- subject area
- overall website structure
- hosting platform
- website framework
- sitemap
- robots.txt
- structured data (Schema.org)
- canonical URLs
- Search Console configuration
- publishing schedule
- approximate number of published articles

Minor differences that naturally occur during normal publishing will be documented when considered relevant.

---

# Independent Variable

The independent variable is the optimization methodology.

Baseline:
- conventional AI-assisted publishing workflow

Targetlytics:
- Targetlytics-guided optimization workflow

---

# Dependent Variables

The study records multiple outcome measures, including when available:

- AI recommendation visibility
- search engine visibility
- indexing status
- impressions
- clicks
- rankings
- citation frequency
- organic traffic
- additional metrics considered relevant during the study

---

# Variables

Additional explanatory variables may be documented whenever they are considered relevant for interpreting the results.

---

# Deviations

Any deviation from the original study protocol will be documented together with:

- date
- description
- rationale
- expected impact on the study

---

# Scope

This document describes the experimental design for this specific longitudinal case study.

It is not intended to define a general methodology for other AI visibility studies.
