# Limitations

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document describes the known limitations of the study.

Its purpose is to identify factors that may influence the interpretation of the results and to provide transparency regarding the scope and constraints of the research.

Recognizing limitations is an essential part of responsible research and helps readers evaluate the findings appropriately.

---

# General Limitations

This study is subject to several general limitations.

These limitations should be considered when interpreting the observations and results.

---

# Study Design

The study follows a longitudinal case study of a single production website.

Consequently, the findings may not be directly applicable to other websites, industries, languages, or markets.

---

# External Factors

Website visibility may be influenced by factors outside the scope of this project.

Examples include:

- search engine algorithm updates
- AI model updates
- changes in user behavior
- seasonal variation
- competitor activity
- broader technological developments

These factors cannot always be isolated or controlled.

---

# Measurement Limitations

Not all aspects of website visibility can necessarily be measured.

Available metrics may change over time as platforms, tools, and reporting methods evolve.

Some measurements may also be unavailable or incomplete.

---

# Data Limitations

The study relies on the data available during the observation period.

Missing, delayed, or incomplete data may affect the interpretation of specific observations.

Any known data quality issues should be documented.

---

# Methodological Limitations

The research methodology may evolve during the project as new information becomes available.

Any methodological changes will be documented in the project records.

---

# Interpretation

Observed relationships should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of causation.

Multiple factors may contribute to observed changes.

Where uncertainty exists, it should be acknowledged explicitly.

---

# Future Validation

The findings from this study may serve as the basis for future research.

Replication using additional websites, industries, or research designs may strengthen or challenge the observations documented in this project.

---

# Scope

This document identifies known limitations of the current study.

Additional limitations may be added if they become apparent during the project.
