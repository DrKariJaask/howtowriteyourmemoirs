# Project Overview

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This repository documents two parallel activities:

1. The development of **HowToWriteYourMemoir.com**, an educational website dedicated to memoir writing.
2. A longitudinal research project that records how website development and optimization relate to changes in online visibility over time.

The repository serves as the project's permanent documentation, recording both the website development process and the associated research activities.

---

# Website

HowToWriteYourMemoirs.com is being developed to help people transform their life experiences into well-written memoirs.

The website aims to provide practical guidance covering the complete memoir-writing process, including:

- finding a story worth telling
- planning a memoir
- organizing memories
- writing compelling narratives
- editing and revising
- publishing options
- working with editors, memoir coaches, and ghostwriters

The objective is to create a comprehensive educational resource for anyone interested in writing memoirs.

---

# Research

The website also functions as the subject of a longitudinal case study.

Throughout the project, significant development activities, optimization efforts, measurements, and observations will be documented.

The study focuses on recording:

- what was implemented
- when it was implemented
- why it was implemented
- what was measured
- what was observed

Interpretation of the collected observations is documented separately from the observations themselves.

---

# Objectives

The project has the following objectives:

- Build a high-quality educational website about memoir writing.
- Document the complete website development process.
- Record significant optimization activities.
- Measure website visibility throughout the project.
- Maintain research documentation suitable for future analysis and potential scientific publication.

---

# Research Principles

The project follows these principles.

## Transparency

Important decisions, implementation activities, measurements, and observations are documented throughout the project.

## Reproducibility

The research process is documented in sufficient detail to allow others to understand and repeat a similar methodology.

## Objectivity

Observations are documented regardless of whether they support or contradict expectations.

## Measurement

Whenever practical, conclusions should be based on documented measurements rather than subjective impressions.

## Continuous Documentation

Documentation is maintained throughout the lifetime of the project instead of being reconstructed afterwards.
