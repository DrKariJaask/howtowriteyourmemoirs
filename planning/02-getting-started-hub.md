# Getting Started Hub

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Getting Started hub is the entry point for visitors who are considering writing a memoir or have just decided to begin.

Its purpose is to answer the most common beginner questions, reduce uncertainty, and guide visitors toward the most appropriate educational resources.

---

# Target Audience

The hub primarily serves:

- first-time memoir writers
- people considering writing a memoir
- visitors unfamiliar with memoir writing
- visitors looking for an overview before diving into specific topics

---

# Visitor Questions

This page should answer questions such as:

- What is a memoir?
- Why should I write a memoir?
- Is my life interesting enough?
- Where do I begin?
- What should I do first?
- How difficult is it to write a memoir?

The page should help visitors feel confident enough to continue.

---

# Objectives

The hub should:

- introduce memoir writing
- explain the purpose of the website
- reduce beginner uncertainty
- provide a logical starting point
- guide visitors to the next step

---

# Initial Structure

1. Hero section
2. Introduction
3. What is a memoir?
4. Why write a memoir?
5. Common beginner questions
6. Featured article
7. Next steps

---

# Featured Article

How to Write a Memoir: A Complete Beginner's Guide

# Future Expansion

As the website grows, this hub may include:

- additional beginner articles
- beginner checklists
- downloadable resources
- FAQs
- recommended books
- writing templates

These additions will be planned after the initial Targetlytics analysis.

---

# Success Criteria

Visitors should leave this page with:

- a clear understanding of memoir writing
- confidence that they can begin
- a clear next step
