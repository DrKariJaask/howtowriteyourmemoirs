# Site Map

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the initial structure of HowToWriteYourMemoirs.com.

The site map establishes the main sections of the website and the role of each section.

It defines the website structure only. It does not determine how many articles will eventually be published.

---

URL:

`/`

# Initial Content Scope

The first version of the website will contain:

- the homepage
- eight main section pages
- one initial article for each main section
- necessary supporting pages

Additional articles will not be planned in detail before the first Targetlytics analysis.

---

# Navigation Structure

The primary navigation should include:

- Getting Started
- Finding Your Story
- Planning
- Writing
- Editing
- Publishing
- Marketing
- Professional Services

Supporting sections may be placed in the main navigation, secondary navigation, or footer according to the final design.

---

# Future Development

The site map may expand based on:

- Targetlytics recommendations
- visitor needs
- analytics
- research observations
- editorial decisions

Future additions should fit logically within the existing structure or be documented before the structure is changed.
